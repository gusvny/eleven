A ticket waits when the limit is reached. The next one starts when a ticket at work finishes, fails, or stops for you.
What counts against the limit
  • Counts: a ticket whose stage is running (an agent is thinking, or a check is running).
  • Does not count: a ticket waiting for your signature, a ticket you paused, and a ticket that is finished. They hold no machine time, so they must not hold a slot.
  • Order: the queue is first in, first out. You can pause a queued ticket or move it up in the Ledger tab.
  • Who can change it: only you. The executor sees the limit and obeys it, and cannot raise it — that is the same rule as permissions.
Why this exists: five tickets at work at once made checks slow and timing tests fail.
