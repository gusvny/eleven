Why this mock exists. The new flow (a ticket opts into the develop buffer: lane_version=develop-buffer-v1, develop_line=develop-buffer) changes three things: what evidence the lane must produce (its own scenarios + the declared fast set, 5-minute ceiling), where it lands (onto the develop line, not main), and who signs (one human signature per promotion, not per ticket). The stage diagram does not change — the buffer is a lane version on the same template, so the lane map looks the same by design. What is missing today is the face that says "this ticket is on the buffer, and here is the line's state".

4 · The states a reader must be able to tell apart

  • buffer ticket, proof not yet clean — needs a failure-free proof round (this is exactly where t261009-80 stood all morning: one load-shaped red per attempt, and the two-part proof that fixes it landed at 11:02)
  • landed, waiting for certification — waiting for certification: the designed end state of a ticket; it holds no slot while waiting
  • certified, waiting for promotion — certified by run-… + sign here
  • frozen — the line is red: no new landings except the repair ticket; named owner and escalation point
  • promoted — the buffer's content is in main, with the signature recorded
  • could not run — not a red: the range grows, nothing opens, and the reason is named (runner_missing, could_not_run)
